# Bug sweep and polish pass, November 2026

Previous work: 2026-10-08. Sweep: 2026-11-06.

## How it was tested

- Served with `python -m http.server` and played in a Playwright browser.
- Covered every room and item, a full game to the win screen (184/184, "Master Adventurer"), death by grue, RESTART, and odd parser input:
  - punctuation and empty input
  - 400-character words
  - prototype names such as `constructor` and `__proto__`
  - bare verbs and multi-word item names
- Checked at 375 px width, keyboard-only, with a screen-reader tree snapshot, WCAG contrast maths and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- `Math.random` was stubbed so grue and troll rolls were repeatable.

## For you to decide (not changed)

Ordered by how much they affect play.

1. **Infocom name and text.** The game prints "Copyright (c) 1981, 1982, 1983 Infocom, Inc. All rights reserved" and uses Zork's title, rooms and phrasing. Since the repo may deploy, decide whether a public build should keep these or be renamed and rewritten.
2. **The troll is optional.** The route cellar → chasm → dome → torch room → Egypt → maze skips him. Only the full 184 needs the kill. A missed swing has no consequence, since combat is a flat 50% roll.
3. **Dead trapdoor death.** "You stumble into darkness and fall to your doom" can never happen, because the living room is always lit.
4. **You can take and examine things in the dark**, including `take all` in the attic. Blocking it would be truer to the genre, but a player who drops the lamp could then softlock.
5. **The grue roll happens on every look and every arrival in the dark** (25%). Typing LOOK twice in the dark is a coin flip.
6. **One-way passages.** These are asymmetric links on the map:
   - altar east → torch room has no way back
   - north of house north → forest
   - the clearing grating drops into the cellar
   - maze 3 south → cellar has no way back
7. **Moves counter.** It counts invalid commands, HELP and SCORE.
8. **Parser gaps:**
   - `take` alone means TAKE ALL.
   - `drop all` and `take lamp and sword` aren't supported.
   - `unlock grating` isn't supported.
   - `open case` says "I don't know how".
   - `take key` with the skeleton key held picks that key.
9. **After death or a win**, SCORE and LOOK are refused ("Type RESTART").
10. **Descriptions repeat or go stale:**
    - The trophy case and rug are listed both as items and in the room text.
    - The rug line stays after the rug is moved.
    - A dropped treasure keeps its original spot text ("sits on a plinth").
11. **Phone:** there are no tap buttons for common commands or history.
12. `favicon.ico` 404s, which shows only in the console.
